01 Distribution · Switzerland

Authorised Swiss distributor
for DDT robots.

Xperbot is the Swiss distribution partner for DDT — Direct Drive Technology, the manufacturer behind the DIABLO and TITA wheel-legged robots and the direct-drive actuator technology underneath them. Wheel-legged platforms combine the speed and efficiency of wheels with the ability to climb, balance and handle terrain that stops a conventional mobile robot.

Buying a robot from overseas is easy. Getting it delivered, conform, supported and repaired in Switzerland is not. That is the part we take on.

  • Sales, quotation and configuration advice
  • Import, customs and Swiss delivery
  • Conformity documentation for the local market
  • Commissioning and operator training
  • Spare parts and warranty handling
  • Support in German, English, Italian and Chinese

02 Consulting

Robotics implementation strategy — from a private user to a corporate fleet.

Most robotics disappointment is a specification failure, not a technology failure. We work on the decision before the purchase: what problem is actually being solved, whether a robot is the right answer, which one, at what cost, and what has to change around it to make it work.

Private & individual

One robot, done properly

For private owners, collectors, makers and family users choosing their first serious machine.

  • Needs and expectation setting
  • Model selection and honest limitations
  • Purchase, setup and safe operation
  • Home or studio integration advice
SME & institution

One scene, proven

For a workshop, warehouse, clinic, hotel, school or municipality automating a first concrete task.

  • Scene and process analysis
  • Business case and ROI modelling
  • Vendor shortlist and tender support
  • Pilot design, measurement and review
Corporate

A fleet, governed

For groups deploying across sites, where the hard parts are standards, governance and change management.

  • Automation roadmap and portfolio triage
  • Multi-site rollout and standardisation
  • Safety, compliance and data governance
  • Workforce training and adoption

How an engagement runs

01

Discovery

Site visit and interviews. We map the process as it really runs, not as the manual describes it.

02

Scene definition

The task, environment and constraints written down in a form a supplier can quote against — the same structured method behind RSIP.

03

Options & business case

Shortlisted technologies with costs, risks, payback and — where it applies — the recommendation not to automate.

04

Pilot & rollout

Supplier selection, pilot supervision, measured acceptance criteria, then scaling with training and support in place.

What you receive
  • A structured requirements document your suppliers can bid against
  • A costed options comparison with risks made explicit
  • ROI model with the assumptions you can challenge
  • Pilot protocol with pass/fail acceptance criteria
  • Implementation roadmap by phase, with owners

We are vendor-neutral in consulting engagements. Where a recommendation touches a product we distribute, we say so in writing and you are free to buy elsewhere.

04 Certification consultancy

Getting a robot legally onto the Swiss and EU market.

Conformity is where excellent robots stall at the border. We guide manufacturers, importers and operators through the route to market: which legislation applies, which standards to design against, what evidence has to exist, and what has to be in the file before anything is placed on the market.

For manufacturers & importers

  • Regulatory gap analysis and applicable-legislation mapping
  • Conformity route and standards selection
  • Risk assessment support to ISO 12100 methodology
  • Technical file structure and documentation review
  • Test-house coordination and lab liaison
  • Instructions, marking and Declaration of Conformity review
  • EU/Swiss authorised-representative and importer duties

For operators & buyers

  • Due diligence on a supplier's conformity claims before you buy
  • Workplace risk assessment for the robot in your actual scene
  • Safety concept for human–robot shared spaces
  • Operator instruction and training documentation
  • Data protection and AI-governance review of deployed systems
  • Modification assessment when you change or add to a machine
Frameworks we work in

EU market

Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and the incoming Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, EMC 2014/30/EU, Radio Equipment 2014/53/EU, Low Voltage 2014/35/EU, RoHS, WEEE, GPSR, the Batteries Regulation and the AI Act where an AI system is in scope.

Switzerland

Product Safety Act (PrSG) and Machinery Ordinance (MaschV), the Swiss–EU Mutual Recognition Agreement, OFCOM/BAKOM radio requirements, SUVA workplace obligations and Swiss data-protection law (revFADP).

Standards

ISO 12100 risk assessment, ISO 10218-1/-2 and ISO/TS 15066 for industrial and collaborative robots, ISO 13482 for personal-care robots, ISO 3691-4 for driverless industrial trucks, plus the relevant functional-safety and EMC standards.

Xperbot provides consultancy and documentation support. We are not a notified body or a testing laboratory — where third-party assessment or accredited testing is required, we identify it early and manage the process with the appropriate body on your behalf.
