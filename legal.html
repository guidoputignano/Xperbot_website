Privacy notice

This notice explains how this website (xperbot.ch) handles personal data, in line with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

This is a static informational website. We do not use contact forms, user accounts, our own cookies, or analytics and advertising tools. We do not sell personal data.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub technically processes your IP address and standard browser information in server logs, as is necessary to deliver the site. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Third-party content

Your rights
