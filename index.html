Program 01 · Platform

Describe the scene.
Get the robot solution.

The bottleneck in robotics is not the hardware — it is that the people who own the problem and the people who own the technology do not share a language. RSIP is the translation layer between them.

  • Guided scene definition — describe the job in business language, not robotics terms.
  • AI matching — requirements decomposed into capabilities, scored against the model database.
  • Digital-twin validation — see the task chain run before anyone signs a purchase order.
  • Deployment report — suitability, safety and compliance assessment you can act on.

Program 02 · Education

Children who understand
how robots become smart.

AAL is Xperbot's education program in Zurich for children aged 6–12. Five levels on real robots, from giving a machine every instruction, to letting it decide, to teaching it how to learn.

  • Five levels, ages 6–12 — each ending in a public finale children present themselves.
  • Think first, logic first — no run starts before the child has spoken a prediction.
  • Documented curriculum — AAL-certified teachers, max 16 children, working in pairs.
  • Evidence of thinking — mission logs, measured results and the 76-unit AAL Passport.

About us

Our story

Xperbot was founded in 2025 in Zug, Switzerland, on a simple observation: robot technology was racing ahead while the organisations that need it were stuck — unable to specify what they wanted, assess what was offered, or clear the regulatory and localisation hurdles in between.

We build the missing layer. Demand-driven, ecosystem-enabled, technology-powered — from requirement analysis and matching, through localisation and conformity, to training and long-term support. Our core value is simple: expertise drives innovation.

Our mission

To empower organisations with accessible, adaptable and actionable robotics implementation that delivers measurable value.

Our vision

A world where intelligent machines seamlessly enhance human potential — because deploying them is no longer the hard part.
